Accessibility

We want this website to be readable, usable with a keyboard, and compatible with assistive technologies. If something gets in your way, please tell us.

Report a barrier

Email contact@libreaccessibility.org. A short description is enough; you do not need to identify a technical cause or a WCAG criterion.

If you can, include:

  • The page address.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened.
  • Your browser, device or assistive technology, if relevant and you are comfortable sharing it.
  • What would have helped, or whether you need the information in another format.

Send only the details needed to explain the barrier. You do not need to share a diagnosis or medical information. If you include a screenshot, remove personal information you do not want to send.

We will review the report and may ask for more information. We cannot promise a resolution date before we understand the issue.

How we handle your report

Your original report and reply details stay private, accessible to authorized reviewers. We use them to understand the barrier, reply and coordinate any agreed work, not for marketing.

If a public summary would help people follow a fix, we will show you the exact text and ask for your affirmative permission first. Silence is not permission. Declining publication does not affect review of your report, and material changes to an approved summary need new permission.

Our privacy and retention policy explains record reviews and removal. For access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of publication permission, email contact@libreaccessibility.org.

Our approach

We use the W3C Web Accessibility Initiative (WAI) as our primary standards reference, alongside the experience of people who use assistive technologies. We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. This is an objective, not a claim that the whole website conforms.

Current limits

  • The project proposal form needs JavaScript and a Cloudflare spam check. If you cannot use it, you can email your proposal to contact@libreaccessibility.org instead.
  • Some links lead to external services, including GitHub and community platforms. We do not control their interfaces. Tell us if one prevents you from participating so we can discuss another way.

Our checks do not establish complete WCAG conformance or compatibility with every assistive technology. Reports from people using the site help us identify barriers we have missed.
