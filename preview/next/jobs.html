Volunteer

Help run LibreAccessibility or contribute to our projects.

  • Board director

    VolunteerLibreAccessibilityGovernance

    We are a new nonprofit and have two board seats open. It takes a few hours each quarter and it's remote.

    To apply, write to contact@libreaccessibility.org.

  • Screen reader tester

    VolunteerRoboDK VoiceAccessTesting

    Test new releases with NVDA or JAWS and report what doesn't work.

    To apply, write to contact@libreaccessibility.org.

POST A VOLUNTEER ROLE

Email contact@libreaccessibility.org with the role and how to apply. The work has to be on open source accessibility.
